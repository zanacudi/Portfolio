Tomar

Records screen, camera and audio in the browser, and saves a single file you can keep.

Consent and privacy: Record only when everyone present has been informed and recording is permitted by your organization and by law. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — the file is produced in your browser and stays on your device.

What to record

To record your screen on this device

Use the recorder built into the operating system — it captures the whole screen, including apps a browser can never see. No web page can do this on a phone: neither Safari on iOS nor Chrome on Android exposes the screen-capture API to web pages. Come back here afterwards if you also want a separate camera or voice recording.

    Save file

    On iPhone and iPad, tap Share / save to device to send the file to Files, Photos or another app. If that button is unavailable, press and hold the video above and choose Save.

    Notes on each mode

    • Screen + audio — desktop only. Use current Edge or Chrome on Windows for system-audio support, and tick Share system audio in the picker or other participants will not be recorded.
    • Camera + mic — works on iPhone, Android and desktop. On phones you can switch between front and rear cameras while recording is stopped.
    • Microphone only — works everywhere and produces a much smaller audio file.

    Output format depends on the browser: Chrome, Edge and Firefox produce WebM; Safari on Mac, iPhone and iPad produces MP4. Both play in the browser they were made in, and both are accepted by common video tools.
