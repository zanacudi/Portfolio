About

I'm a Professional Engineer (licensed in Colorado and Nevada) and PhD candidate in Geotechnical Engineering at Colorado State University, currently working as a Geotechnical Engineer at AECOM. My background spans field investigation (CPT soundings, piezometer installation, drilling, borehole logging), numerical modeling for tailings and hydraulic dams, and earthquake geotechnical engineering analysis. I'm comfortable across a wide range of geotechnical and civil software — GeoStudio, PLAXIS, FLAC, UTEXAS, RocScience, AutoCAD Civil 3D, ArcGIS — and use programming tools like MATLAB, Python, C++, and R for analysis and automation. Alongside my engineering work, I teach and mentor: I've been a teaching and research assistant at CSU since 2017, tutored engineering students internationally, and run a geotechnical engineering education page with 12,000+ followers.
