Critical State Soil Mechanics — Interactive Mind Map

The whole framework on one map, drawn the way Buzan intended: a central idea, curved branches, one keyword each. Every equation comes with a table of its symbols — what each one means and the units it carries — and most ideas come with a sketch. Drag the detail slider to go from bare keywords to formulas to full explanations — and click any branch to open it, and the ones marked • carry a live calculator. Nothing here assumes you already know the subject. Built after M. Rama Rao & P. Samatha Chowdary, Geotechnical Engineering Through Mind Maps (2019), using the conventions in their Section 1.4. Prepared by Zana Taher.
